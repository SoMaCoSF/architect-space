01

The Shape

your names first-class · ours in gold

Your post named the boundary precisely: Jev is not intended to hold authority over the exchange — it is the structured decision layer. LedgerGuard remains the deterministic policy boundary for anything that can materially affect the platform. That is the architecture we ship. The mapping:

Your termOur termWhat it means, shared
LedgerGuardPDP GATE The deterministic policy boundary. Every proposed act climbs it. It checks act class, ceiling, scope, value, and forbidden acts — and its outcome, not the model’s confidence, is what authorizes.
Guardian / SentinelBOUNDED ACTOR An actor with a capability ceiling carried in its identity: allowed act classes, max value at risk, allowed scopes. A sentinel may monitor, restrict, quarantine, reroute, escalate — never expand its own permissions, move treasury funds, bypass limits, or override hard controls.
Decision-receiptWITNESS LEDGER Every material proposal, gate decision, and act result appends a hash-chained receipt. The audit trail is the decision record — replayable, not reconstructed from logs after the fact.
Jev decision + confidenceTYPED PROPOSAL A finite choice, a confidence, a distribution over alternatives, and evidence references — wrapped in a decision envelope with its own typed identity. The classifier proposes. It never writes to the actuator path.
Bounded authorityCEILINGS INTERSECT Actor, parent, venue, and session ceilings combine by intersection, never union. A delegated or spawned sentinel inherits a subset — authority only attenuates down the lineage. Freeze is a narrow defensive act class; unfreeze is a separate, stronger act the sentinel does not hold.
Route / provider / anomalyTYPED SUBJECTS Routes, providers, actors, decisions, and receipts each carry typed UUID identities. An identity is an address for correlation — never a credential. Authority comes from policy and gate outcome, never from possessing an ID.
The gray band is the honest middle. Below the confidence floor a proposal is denied or observe-only. Inside the band it escalates to a human or higher guardian — it does not become a weak yes. Above the band it may proceed to deterministic checks. It still does not authorize by itself.

Receipt Schema, Side by Side

click a step — see the receipt it wrote

Your five receipt fields against the harness receipt, as actually written by guardian.py. Then run the worked scenario: a degraded ETH–USDC route, classified, gated, frozen — three receipts, chain-verified. The counter-cases below are gate self-checks: the gate denied or escalated before any act, so no act receipt exists — which is the point.

Yours — the five fields

  • what was observed
  • what Jev decided
  • confidence / distribution
  • what policy allowed or denied
  • the final action taken

Ours — where each field lives

  • evidence[] on the proposal receipt — failures_5m, p95_ms, anomaly_score
  • choice on the proposal receipt — the classifier’s finite decision
  • confidence + distribution{} on the proposal receipt
  • verdict + reason on the gate-decision receipt
  • result{status} on the act-result receipt

Plus the chain itself: every receipt carries seq, kind, prev_hash, and its own hash — each receipt commits to the one before it, back to GENESIS. Tamper with any field and every later hash breaks. Typed identities (decision:…, actor:…, route:…) correlate the chain without acting as credentials.

Harness, Verified

stdlib only · ran on our machine · 2026-10-06

The harness is deliberately small: a typed decision envelope, a policy gate, ceiling intersection, gray-band routing, a deterministic actuator, and the hash-chained ledger. The classifier inside is a deterministic stub behind the typed envelope — labeled as such in its own output (classifier: "stub:deterministic-rules-v1"). Live Jev swaps in behind the same envelope without touching the gate. What is proven here is the authority boundary, not model accuracy.

What this does not claim: no production DEX integration, no claim the classifier is accurate, no UUID-as-credential anywhere. A hash chain is tamper-evidence inside the ledger; production still needs signer and external-anchoring choices matched to the threat model. Those are named future work in the design note, not fine print.

Use it. The harness is public — feed your own classifier’s output into the gate and prove what happened: github.com/SoMaCoSF/architect-space/tree/main/harness. Stdlib only, no dependencies, no network. The classifier in the tree is the same labeled stub — what you are testing is the boundary, not a model. Three commands:

python3 cli.py run scenarios/honest-route-freeze.json --ledger ledger.jsonl python3 cli.py verify ledger.jsonl python3 cli.py check

run gates one scenario — your actor, your ceilings, your classifier’s finished proposal — and appends the chained receipts. verify recomputes the chain and names the first broken receipt. check is the conformance suite; every case is named, every failure is loud:

ITPM × Imbue, Inside This Shape

the same discipline, two shipped forms

ITPM — who signs

ITPM is our activation platform for large capital builds — hospitals and technology spaces delivered through trade lanes with hard gates between them. Its contribution to this shape is the activation discipline: every act belongs to a lane, every lane has an owner, and the next phase does not unlock until the interface before it is green with evidence attached and signed by the role that owns it. Translated here: a freeze, a quarantine, an escalation are not log lines — they are gated acts with a named signer and evidence, or they did not happen.

Imbue — the shipped proof

Imbue is our desktop command deck, and this pattern already runs inside it in production software: governed acts climb a policy decision point, are witnessed in a ledger before state moves, and roll back only against records whose digest still matches the witness. Signed posture packs stage a diff, apply through the gate, and snapshot first. The Guardian/Sentinel harness is that same decide → witness → act spine, pointed at routes and providers instead of files. The pattern is not a slide; it is shipping code with a test suite behind it.
